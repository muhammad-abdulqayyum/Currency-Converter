# Bundled illustrations

## Black American Heritage Flag

Flag designed in 1967 by Melvin Charles and Gleason T. Jackson. SVG rendition by Xophe84, CC0 1.0 Universal, rendered and resized to PNG for Android.

Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Black_American_Heritage_flag.svg
Original SVG: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/f/ff/Black_American_Heritage_flag.svg
License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

## Globe

Natural Earth public-domain land geography, distributed by world-atlas 2.0.2 (land-110m.json), converted to an equirectangular land mask. Orthographic projection and lighting rendered locally in the app.

https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
https://github.com/topojson/world-atlas

Metal bars and Bitcoin coin are original canvas illustrations.


## Crypto identification artwork (4.0.3)

The small ETH, LTC and XRP identification icons are locally drawn vectors in `artwork/crypto/`, rendered into matching bundled images for Android and iOS. They depict the recognizable Ethereum diamond, Litecoin Ł, and community XRP X symbol. They identify conversion assets and do not imply endorsement. Reference materials: https://ethereum.org/en/assets/ , https://litecoin.com/resources , https://xrpl.org/docs/introduction/what-is-xrp . Subunit badges are app labels, not separate coin logos.
